Should You Choose a Male or Female African Grey?

Talking & mimicryCompare
MaleOften a touch more talkative and a bolder mimic
FemaleSlightly quieter, but still an excellent talker
TemperamentCompare
MaleConfident; can be territorial or dominant, especially in breeding season
FemaleTypically gentler and more even, though it can be moody at times
Bonding styleCompare
MaleOutgoing — often bonds with the whole household
FemaleMay bond closely with one person; can be reserved with strangers
Telling them apartFact

African Greys are sexually monomorphic — males and females look identical — so PCR-based DNA sexing from a feather or blood sample, not invasive surgical sexing, is the only certain method. It is included with every bird we place.
