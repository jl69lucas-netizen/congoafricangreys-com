Should You Choose a Male or Female African Grey?

Male African Grey

  • Talking & mimicryOften a touch more talkative and a bolder mimic
  • TemperamentConfident; can be territorial or dominant, especially in breeding season
  • Bonding styleOutgoing — often bonds with the whole household

Female African Grey

  • Talking & mimicrySlightly quieter, but still an excellent talker
  • TemperamentTypically gentler and more even, though it can be moody at times
  • Bonding styleMay bond closely with one person; can be reserved with strangers
Telling them apart

African Greys are sexually monomorphic — males and females look identical — so PCR-based DNA sexing from a feather or blood sample, not invasive surgical sexing, is the only certain method. It is included with every bird we place.
