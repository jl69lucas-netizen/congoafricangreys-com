What Is a Congo African Grey — the Classic Red-Tailed Talker?

The Congo is the larger of the two Grey species and the bird most people picture when they think of a parrot that talks.

Very well, and in a voice that sounds startlingly human. The Congo is the bolder, more broadcast-clear talker of the two Greys — it picks up whole phrases, mimics the microwave and the dog, and learns which words get a reaction. Ours come out of the nest box into our hands in the family home, so the first voice they copy is usually one of ours.

What Is a Timneh African Grey — the Calmer, Earlier-Talking Species?

Because the Timneh forgives more. It is the smaller, darker Grey — maroon tail, horn-colored upper beak — and in our home it has been the steadier of the two: less thrown by a new visitor or a moved cage, quicker to settle, and often the first to try a word. That is why it is the Grey we point first-time owners and busy households toward.
