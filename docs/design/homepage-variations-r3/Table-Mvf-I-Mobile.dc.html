Talking & mimicry

Male African Grey · Often a touch more talkative and a bolder mimic

Female African Grey · Slightly quieter, but still an excellent talker

Temperament

Male African Grey · Confident; can be territorial or dominant, especially in breeding season

Female African Grey · Typically gentler and more even, though it can be moody at times

Bonding style

Male African Grey · Outgoing — often bonds with the whole household

Female African Grey · May bond closely with one person; can be reserved with strangers

African Greys are sexually monomorphic — males and females look identical — so PCR-based DNA sexing from a feather or blood sample, not invasive surgical sexing, is the only certain method. It is included with every bird we place.
