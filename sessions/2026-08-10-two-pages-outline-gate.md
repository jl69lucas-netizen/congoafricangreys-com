## D · Open Flags for this gate

1. **The header dup-gate cannot run against an unbuilt page.** `dup_content_audit.py --headers` compares `dist/` output. What ran here is the **pre-write** half of the discipline: every proposed header was checked as a string against the built cluster (§E). The mechanical gate runs at Task 10 on the built pages, and a failure there means the outline was wrong, not just the prose.
2. **Page A H2 2 and Page B H2 5 are the closest pair on the site** by subject. They are deliberately worded apart, and the evidence must be *presented* differently — Page A as an explanation of our own pricing, Page B as a warning about someone else's listings. Highest dup-gate risk on either page; write these two from their own outlines and never side by side.
3. **Page B H1 variant 1 and H2 5 stand or fall together.** If the scam section is cut, "Not a Classified Ad" is an unsupported claim in the H1 and the H1 must change with it.
