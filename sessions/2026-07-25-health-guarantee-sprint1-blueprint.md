## 1. Two strategies + one blended (Recommend + Why + trade-off)

**Strategy 1 — "Catalogue-First."** Lead with bird cards + prices; guarantee as a trust badge. *Pro:* fastest first-scroll conversion. *Con:* forfeits the trust/scam intent that is *already ranking this URL at pos 11*, and loses to the deeper SERP intent — the whole SERP is trust-shaped (Sprint 0 §4). Rejected.

**Strategy 2 — "Trust-Authority First."** Lead with the full guarantee reframe + scam teardown + documentation; birds far down. *Pro:* maximal AEO/trust ownership. *Con:* buries the transactional layer like the DNA page did — but DNA had *zero* demand to protect; this page already ranks and converts, so a long preamble needlessly delays the cards. Rejected as-is.

**Blended — "Reframe-then-Reserve" (RECOMMENDED).** A tight trust reframe (what a real guarantee covers + the 72-hr-vs-1-year reframe) + 6 counter snippets + the guarantee-terms table up top to *own the intent*, then **bird cards immediately after** (the page already ranks — no long preamble needed), then documentation → scam-answer → EEAT → FAQ → form. **Why:** it defends the exact trust moat that ranks the page while surfacing the transactional layer higher than DNA. **Trade-off (named):** the top ~two sections persuade before they sell, so first-scroll "enquire" clicks are slightly slower than a bare grid — accepted because the traffic is trust-driven and the moat is what climbs pos-11 → top-5.
