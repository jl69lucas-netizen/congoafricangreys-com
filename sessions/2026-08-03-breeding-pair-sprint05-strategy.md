## 1 · Strategy A — "The Standard"

**Angle.** Own the word *proven*. The page is the category's reference definition; the birds are the
proof that we meet it. Definition first, market audit second, stock third.

**Query focus.** Head term + the definitional long-tail (*what does proven mean on a breeding pair*,
*at what age can african greys breed*, *bonded pair vs proven pair*).

**H2 architecture (abridged).** What "Proven" Actually Means on a Breeding Pair → Why Most Pairs
Advertised as Proven Are Not → How Old a Grey Must Be Before It Can Breed → What the Market Is Selling
You Instead → The Three Pairs We Can Prove → What Paperwork Proves It → Reserve.

**Conversion path.** Trust → standard → stock. Slow, high-qualification. The buyer arrives convinced.

**Risk.** **Intent mismatch.** The SERP is entirely commercial (§0.2). Leading with 800 words of
definition on a "for sale" query risks pogo-sticking at exactly the moment we are trying to move
7.14 → top 3. It also buries the one asset nobody else has — named birds — below the fold.

---

## 2 · Strategy B — "The Ladder"

**Angle.** Three real pairs, priced by the breeding years they have left. The page is a buying guide
for breeder stock: which pair fits your aviary, your timeline and your budget.

**Query focus.** Head term + transactional (*proven pair for sale*, *bonded pair african grey price*).

**H2 architecture (abridged).** The Three Proven Pairs You Can Reserve → Why the Pair With Fewer
Clutches Costs More → What Each Pair Has Produced → What a Proven Pair Costs Elsewhere → How a Pair
Ships → Reserve.

**Conversion path.** Stock → price logic → paperwork → reserve. Fast, high-intent, matches the SERP.

**Risk.** **Thin against the classifieds.** On pure commercial terms we are three pairs against
birdsnow's eighteen, with less domain authority. Nothing on the page is structurally uncopyable, and
hookbillsforsale — a classified with a deep species guide — already outranks pure product pages at #12.
Commercial-only concedes the one axis we can win.

## 3 · Strategy C — "Three Pairs, One Standard" · **(Recommended)**

**Angle.** Lead commercial, because the query is commercial — but make **every commercial element
evidence for the standard**. The three pairs appear above the fold *with their clutch counts visible*,
which makes the definition unavoidable: the reader sees "4 clutches hatched" before they read what
*proven* means, so the definition arrives as an explanation of what they are already looking at rather
than as a lecture that delays the price.

**The structural idea in one line:** *product-as-proof-of-standard* — not definition-first (A), not
product-first (B), but a product presentation that can only be honest if the standard is stated.

**Query focus.** Head term and transactional variants carry the ranking; the definitional long-tail
carries the moat and the AEO/LLM citations.

**Conversion path.** Named stock → the number that needs explaining (clutches) → the standard →
the ladder → market comparison → risk disclosure → paperwork → reserve. The buyer is qualified *by*
the sales content, not before it.

**Why this beats A and B, grounded:**

| Evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|
| All 14 SERP results commercial; head term already at 7.14 | Must open transactional — rules out A |
| birdsnow 18 ads + per-state index; we have 3 pairs | Cannot win on volume — rules out B |
| Only named stock in a 44-domain registry | Lead with the asset nobody can copy |
| hookbillsforsale ranks #12 on depth with **zero** pair content | Depth ranks here; the moat is real |
| royalbirdcompany holds the honest position but is `noindex` | The honest position is **unoccupied in search** |
| 4.63% CTR at position 7 | The commercial snippet already works — keep it |

**Trade-off, named plainly.** Three costs, and they are real:

1. **It is the most expensive page in the cluster to build.** Two layers interleaved, not stacked.
   If it degrades into "product page, then FAQ," it loses both advantages and becomes Strategy B with
   extra words.
2. **Publishing the standard constrains us permanently.** Once we define *proven* as *has hatched a
   fertile clutch*, we can never market an unproven pair as proven — on this page or any future one.
   That is a real, ongoing restriction on how future stock can be sold.
3. **It will talk some buyers out of a purchase.** Stating that breeder stock is not tame, that
   relocation can stop production, and that pairing is not guaranteed will cost inquiries. Consistent
   with the congo-pair decision, where the step-down was ruled a feature.

**The alternative I rejected outright.** Chasing Cluster E (`african grey breeders`, ~462 impressions at
position 50–99). It is bigger than this page's own cluster and tempting, but it is **find-a-breeder**
intent and belongs to the breeders-comparison page. Taking it here would put two of our URLs on one
intent — the exact failure the 2026-07-30 cannibalization guard was created to prevent. **One link out,
nothing more.**

## 5 · Tool / special element decision

**Recommendation: the "Is This Pair Actually Proven?" verification checklist.**

Six questions the buyer runs against **any** seller's listing — ours included: *Has this pair hatched a
fertile clutch, and when? · How old is each bird? · Are both closed-banded, and what are the numbers? ·
Can you see the clutch record? · Is the pair sold together? · Will the seller state what happens if the
pair stops producing after the move?*

**Evidence for it:** the fan-out contains *what does proven mean*, *how old must a breeding pair be*,
*african grey parrot breeding cage size* (**already position 1**, 1 impression) and *african grey nest box for sale* —
all checklist-shaped. No SERP result ships any interactive element. It fuses the definitional moat with
the anti-scam cluster and, because it works on competitors' listings, it is linkable and shareable in a
way a product widget never is.

**Rejected:** a productive-years calculator. It serves only our own three pairs and duplicates
information better shown as a static figure on each pair card. **One tool per page** — remaining
productive years becomes a **card element**, not a second interactive module.

Pure HTML/CSS + minimal vanilla JS. No frameworks, no CDNs. Live figures read from `price-matrix.json`.

## 6 · CTA strategy

- **Cadence:** a reserve CTA every 500–700 words, anchored to `#reserve`.
- **One global CTA:** the page ships its own band, so `hideGlobalCta` is set.
- **Anchor text — branded action anchors, none of them spent** (checked against the Anchor Diversity
  Ledger): *Reserve a proven pair* · *Ask for the clutch record* · *See what each pair has produced* ·
  *Talk to Mark about breeder stock*.
- **Form:** must list all three pairs with their real prices, plus the available singles and fertile
  eggs, plus the Midland pickup option alongside the $185 / $350 tiers.
- **Honest scarcity only:** three pairs, stated as three. No countdowns, no fabricated urgency.
- **Deliberate step-down path:** buyers who want a companion, not breeder stock, get a clear route to
  the single-bird pages and to the companion-pair sibling — framed as fit, not as a lesser option.
