## 0 · The headline finding — Google's own AI Overview calls our price fraudulent

Fetched live 2026-08-10, `google.com/search?q=buy+african+grey+parrot+online`, stealth proxy, US. The AI
Overview renders **above every organic result** and says, verbatim:

> *"dozens of slick websites offering African Greys for $800 to $1,500 are absolute internet scams targeting bird buyers."*
> *"**Unrealistic prices:** Real hand-fed, DNA-tested African Greys cost thousands; **prices under $2,000 are nearly always fraudulent**."*
> *"**Congo Greys:** Usually cost between $8,000 and $8,550 from reputable hand-feeding breeders. **Timneh Greys:** Slightly smaller and often priced around $6,500."*
> *"prices typically ranging from $5,000 to $8,500"*

**Three of our six available birds are priced inside the band Google's AI Overview tells buyers is "nearly

searches the exact term this page is being retargeted at reads that warning *before* they reach any result.

This is not a keyword problem and it cannot be fixed with copy alone. It is logged as **Open Flag 1** below

YouTube channel and a listings platform — which is the opening, and §5 develops it.

## 5 · Gap analysis — what this page can own

### 5a · The unclaimed citation slot
The AI Overview cites a YouTube channel, a listings platform, and a retail aviary. **No breeder site with
published documentation holds a citation slot.** A page that publishes verifiable, checkable specifics —
USDA AWA number, CITES Appendix I paperwork, DNA lab, named birds with individual prices — is competing for a
slot nobody currently occupies.

### 5b · The price-artifact proof
Nobody on this SERP explains *why* prices range from $850 to $8,500. We can, from live measured evidence
(§3c) plus sweep 3 §A2. This is a moat: a scam site cannot publish it, and an aggregator will not.

### 5c · The guarantee asymmetry, answered rather than dodged
`buyafricangreyparrots.com` advertises 1 year; ours is 72 hours / 3 days. The honest and stronger frame is
**enforceability** — the same lever the health-guarantee page already uses. A long guarantee from an
anonymous storefront and a short one from a named breeder at a verifiable Midland address are not the same
instrument. **The page must name our own term plainly first and make the argument second**; the Verified-Claim
Ledger bounds what may be said about either.

### 5d · The `usa` / `online` qualifier
The local pack owns the unqualified head term. Our winnable set is the qualified one. Both are in autosuggest
and in people-also-search-for; neither is targeted anywhere on our site today.

### 5e · Structural depth
Every ranking page tops out at H3. Our gate requires six levels with ≥5 H5 and ≥5 H6.
