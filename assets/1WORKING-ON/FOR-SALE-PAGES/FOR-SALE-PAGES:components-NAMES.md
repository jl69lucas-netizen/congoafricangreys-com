### **Raising a Grey — *they're smarter than you think***

African Greys are arguably the most intelligent companion bird. Success comes from understanding their psychology, not forcing routines.

**01**

#### **Positive reinforcement**

Greys are sensitive and shut down with harsh corrections. Reward, never punish.  
**02**

#### **Short training sessions**

10–15 minute sessions, twice daily, work better than long ones. Quit while they're winning.  
**03**

#### **Consistent commands**

Same words. Same tone. Every time. Grey memory rewards consistency relentlessly.  
**04**

#### **Mental enrichment daily**

Foraging toys, puzzles, and rotated novelty. A bored Grey is a destructive Grey.  
**05**

#### **Early socialization**

Expose to varied people, sounds, and environments in the first six months — confidence for life.  
🦜  
**Need a hand?**

**01**

#### **Positive reinforcement only**

Reward what you want repeated. Greys are sensitive — punishment creates fearful, plucking birds.  
**02**

#### **Short, daily sessions**

**04**

#### **Out-of-cage hours**

Minimum 3–4 supervised hours daily. The cage is a sleeping perch, not a holding pen.

### **Six things every first-time Grey owner gets wrong.**

We send this list with every chick. If you read nothing else from us, read this.

**01**  
**Free-flight too early**  
Trim wings until trust \+ recall are rock solid. Six months minimum.  
**02**  
**Seed-only diet**  
Causes fatty-liver disease. Pellets \+ chop, seed as occasional treat.  
**03**  
**Over-bonding to one person**  
Rotate handling across the household from day one.  
**04**  
**No annual avian-vet visit**  
Birds hide illness. Bloodwork yearly catches what behavior won't.  
**05**  
**Teflon & aerosols**  
Non-stick fumes kill birds within minutes. Cast iron, period.  
**06**  
**Punishing screams**  
You reward it with attention. Ignore until calm, then reward calm.

### **Common questions**

Quick answers — tap any question to expand. For details, the full owner's guide ships with every bird.

**How long do African Greys live?\+**

40–60 years in good conditions. Plan accordingly — this is a multi-decade commitment.  
**What do they eat?\+**

High-quality pellets (60%) \+ fresh chop daily. Seeds as occasional treats only.  
**Are they loud?\+**

Quieter than most parrots — but they have a "contact call" in the morning and at dusk.  
**Cage size required?\+**

Minimum 36"W × 24"D × 48"H for an adult Congo. Bigger is always better.  
**Do they bite?\+**

Rarely, if well-socialised. Always for a reason — read body language, never punish.  
**Cost per year after purchase?\+**

\~$600–$1,200/yr: food, toys, vet checks. Plus emergency vet budget.
